Cyber Crime Complaint Management System

Common Types of Cyber Crimes

Credit Card Fraud⬇️
Unauthorized use of someone’s credit or debit card information to make purchases or withdraw money.
Cyberbullying⬇️
Harassment or threats through social media, messaging platforms or online channels.
Data Breach⬇️
Unauthorized access to confidential data such as passwords, banking details or personal information.
Fake Website Scam⬇️
Fraudulent websites created to steal login credentials or trick users into fake payments.
Hacking⬇️
Illegal access to systems or networks to steal, alter or destroy information.
Identity Theft⬇️
Stealing personal details like Aadhaar, PAN or bank information to commit fraud.
Online Fraud⬇️
Financial scams via fake job offers, lottery scams or fraudulent investments.
Phishing⬇️
Fraudulent emails or messages tricking users into revealing passwords or OTPs.
Ransomware⬇️
Malware that encrypts data and demands payment to restore access.
Social Media Abuse⬇️
Misuse of social networking platforms for harassment, fake profiles or defamation.
